About me

I'm a software engineer based in Porto, doing a master's in Software Engineering at FEUP. I mostly work on the backend, and I like systems that are simple to reason about and easy to change.

I've built Go services for a raffle platform with 10,000+ users and zero ticket collisions, and designed the backend and infrastructure of a platform the Brazilian government uses to manage renovations in 900 public schools. Along the way I co-authored two papers on LLMs and microservice architecture, one of them awarded Best Paper at SBCARS. Right now I'm looking for my next backend role.
